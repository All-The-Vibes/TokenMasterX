  recovery and trace finalization. A per-request process that discards recovery
  state is not a completed Headroom integration.
- Do not import benchmark runners, hidden oracles, or fixture execution into
  the installed production path.
- Apply at most one context-reduction engine to a given representation.
  Shared instructions, source provenance, tool/result pairing, exact/prefix
  protections and final transport guards stay authoritative.
- Retain original client history and exact source/recovery data. No-op and
  unsupported protocol/capability cases must be explicit.
- Ponytail-style instruction overhead and Headroom/local recovery overhead
  count. Do not multiply separately advertised savings or always enable an
  extra prompt that has not earned its overhead.
- Jev never supplies truth labels, authorization, or its own promotion
  certificate. Keep advisory calls bounded and sanitized; use only explicitly
  configured credentials, never persist or echo secrets.
- Preserve the existing strict quality/promotion rules. Keep deterministic
  reconstruction/size guarantees separate from measured model behavior and
  statistical claims.
- No new dependency, global host/profile/routing change, destructive action,
  or external deployment is authorized merely by this plan. Use existing
  components and isolated/project-local execution first.

## Measurement and decision contract

- Primary: gateway-reported input plus output tokens over every task attempt,
  including recovery requests. Missing usage is unknown, never zero.
- Secondary: fixed-tokenizer counts of complete captured API bodies, explicitly
  labeled payload/reference counts rather than authenticated serving usage.
- Show task correctness, model-request counts, recovery counts, per-task totals,
  per-repetition totals, and pooled descriptive totals.
- Report readiness requests separately. Learning and auxiliary inference are
  not silently folded into task totals or asserted free. Jev and policy search
  are disabled for this study.
- Keep failed, incomplete, and budget-stopped attempts and planned-but-missing
  runs. Do not replace failures, retry selected tasks, or retune after results.
- A positive suite comparison requires complete known usage, no paired quality
  regressions, every candidate answer passing, and lower total reported tokens
  in each of the three repetitions.
- A paired quality regression is a worse result. At equal paired correctness,
  consistently higher token totals are also worse. Trade-offs, ties, missing
  usage, and inconsistent directions are inconclusive.
- These labels describe the frozen suites only. They are not statistical
  noninferiority, population savings, or a production promotion certificate.

## Scope boundaries

recovery schemas are retained and clarified equally in all arms. This does not
fix, rerank, or claim parity with Headroom's earlier failed run.
